Example 1

For a particular power plant the heat added and rejected both occur at constant temperature and no other processes experience any heat transfer. The heat is added in the amount of 3150 kJ at 440ºC and is rejected in the amount of 1950 kJ at 20ºC. Is the Clausius Inequality satisfied and is the cycle reversible or irreversible?













Clausius Inequality is satisfied. Since the inequality is less than zero, the cycle has at least one irreversible process and the cycle is irreversible.
