Turbine

Since the fluid pressure drops as the fluid flows through the turbine, dP < 0, and the specific volume is always greater than zero, wrev, turbine > 0. To perform the integral, the pressure-volume relation must be known for the process.

Compressor and Pump

Since the fluid pressure rises as the fluid flows through the compressor or pump, dP > 0, and the specific volume is always greater than zero, wrev, compresor or pump < 0, or wrev, in > 0, or work is supplied to the compressor or pump. To perform the integral, the pressure-volume relation must be known for the process.

The term compressor is usually applied to the compression of a gas.
The term pump is usually applied to increasing the pressure of a liquid.
